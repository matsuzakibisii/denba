Does “Self-Care Only Counts If You Make Dedicated Time for It” Really Hold Up? What Recent Insights Teach Us About ‘Multitasking Care’ Habits and Calf Care

Published: July 18, 2026  |  Source published: June 21, 2026

During rainy season and the hottest stretches of summer, opportunities to go out tend to decrease, and more time is spent at home. Many people probably find themselves putting off self-care, feeling that “it only counts if I make dedicated time for it.” In June 2026, LivelyLife Co., Ltd. (Tachikawa, Tokyo), which plans and sells home appliances and self-care products, drew attention by proposing a cordless-compatible “calf care device” that can be used while watching TV or reading. Prompted by this initiative, here's a look at “multitasking care” habits that fit naturally into daily life, useful for midlife readers and beyond.

Three Points This Proposal Makes

  • A key feature highlighted is a design that fits into “in-between moments” — while watching TV or reading — without requiring you to set aside dedicated time. This is positioned as a way to keep up self-care without strain, even on busy days.
  • A cordless model is available that can be used anywhere, without worrying about outlet locations or cords, adapting to your lifestyle, the explainer notes. Rechargeable for repeated use, it's said to be usable in the living room, a home office, or wherever you prefer.
  • It's proposed as a self-care habit for a variety of everyday moments — breaks during long desk work or standing work, or after exercise. It's introduced not as a special event, but as care that fits naturally into daily life.

What We, in Midlife, Should Keep in Mind Day to Day

What this initiative conveys is that self-care doesn't require special time or a special place — it's about making use of small, everyday in-between moments. The following commonly recommended tips may offer a starting point for revisiting your daily self-care habits.

  • Start easily with whatever fits into a “multitask,” such as while watching TV or reading
  • When you've held the same posture for a long stretch, deliberately set aside time to move or stretch your legs
  • If trying a device, start with short sessions within a comfortable range

* The above are commonly recommended self-care habits and do not guarantee improvement of any specific condition or a specific effect.
