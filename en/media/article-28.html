Is “Protein Only Comes From Protein Shakes” Just an Assumption? What Recent Insights Teach Us About Adding a Protein Habit to Your ‘Everyday Drink’ and Balanced Nutrition

Published: July 19, 2026  |  Source published: July 07, 2026

Protein is considered an essential nutrient for building muscles, skin, hair, and more — but many people probably picture “a protein shake” as the way to get enough of it. In July 2026, Earth Color Co., Ltd. (Shibuya, Tokyo) drew attention by introducing a powdered health drink that focuses on the familiar, everyday drinks barley tea and other teas, letting you be mindful of protein through your usual beverage. Prompted by this initiative, here's a look at building a protein habit as a simple extension of everyday hydration, useful for midlife readers and beyond.

Three Points This Initiative Makes

  • The idea of pairing protein with familiar barley tea is introduced as a way to be mindful of protein at your regular hydration moments, without any special preparation. A feature highlighted is that it can be added just by dissolving it in water, with no shaker or other special tools required.
  • Designed with reduced caffeine and sweetness, it's described as easy to work into any time of day or situation. It's positioned as adaptable to a wide range of everyday moments — during meals, while out, or during relaxation time.
  • The initiative is introduced as broadening protein's image from “something for people who exercise” to a nutrition habit worth being mindful of at any age. It's explained as an idea aimed at people without a dedicated exercise habit, too.

What We, in Midlife, Should Keep in Mind Day to Day

What this initiative conveys is a shift away from assuming “protein has to come from a protein shake,” toward being mindful of it little by little through everyday meals and drinks. The following commonly recommended tips may offer a starting point for revisiting your everyday nutrition habits.

  • At each meal, try to include at least one protein-containing food, such as egg, fish, soy products, or dairy
  • Use hydration moments as one opportunity to be mindful of protein
  • Even on days you don't exercise, keep up the habit of being mindful of protein intake as you age

* The above are commonly recommended dietary habits and do not guarantee improvement of any specific condition or a specific effect. Please consult a medical institution or specialist regarding appropriate intake if needed.
